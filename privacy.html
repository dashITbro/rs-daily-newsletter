Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Purpose

RS Daily Newsletter Importer is a personal application that processes trading newsletters from the owner's Gmail account.

Information Accessed

The application requests read-only Gmail access to identify and retrieve relevant newsletter messages.

How Information Is Used

Newsletter content is processed locally to extract trading support and resistance levels.

Data Storage

Extracted trading levels and authentication tokens are stored locally on the owner's Windows computer. The application does not require a separate application server.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. It does not intentionally transmit Gmail message content to third-party services.

Data Retention

Locally stored trading levels and authentication information are retained until removed by the owner.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
